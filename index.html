01 / Overview

Extraordinary claims. Human experiences.

Alien abduction accounts describe people believing they were taken by non-human beings, often following unexplained lights or a period of missing time. The experiences can feel real and unsettling. Their interpretation remains contested, and no account alone establishes an extraterrestrial cause.

This guide describes reported experiences and possible explanations. It does not treat the cases as confirmed extraterrestrial events.

Report / 01

Missing time

Some accounts describe a gap between a remembered sighting and the next clear memory.

One possible explanation: Memory is reconstructive. Stress, fatigue and later retelling can affect how a period of time is recalled.

A possible explanation is not a diagnosis of any individual report.

05 / Perspective

Other ways to understand the experience.

These topics can help evaluate an account without dismissing the person who reports it.

Sleep paralysis

Temporary inability to move while waking or falling asleep may occur with vivid imagery and a sensed presence.

Memory reconstruction

Memory is not a perfect recording. Expectation, later information and retelling can influence recall.

Cultural influence

Familiar images from films, television and folklore may shape the interpretation of an ambiguous event.

Suggestion under hypnosis

Hypnosis can increase confidence in a memory without establishing its accuracy. Leading questions may influence the account.
