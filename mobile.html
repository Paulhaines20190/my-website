01 / Overview

What are UFO abductions?

UFO abductions (also called alien abductions) refer to claims that people have been taken against their will by non-human beings, usually associated with unidentified flying objects. Reports often describe being lifted into a craft, examined, and then returned with little or no memory of the event.

These stories have been told for decades and form a major part of modern UFO lore, even though mainstream science has not confirmed any of them.

02 / Reports

Common features reported

Missing time

Hours that cannot be accounted for.

Unusual lights

Bright lights or unusual craft in the sky.

Physical sensation

Feelings of paralysis or being watched.

Examinations

Medical-style examinations by small beings.

Physical traces

Strange marks, scars, or implants afterward.

Recovered memories

Vivid dreams or memories recalled under hypnosis.

03 / Case files

Famous cases

CASE 001

Betty & Barney Hill

1961 · New Hampshire

One of the earliest and best-known abduction reports. The couple claimed they were stopped on a New Hampshire highway, taken aboard a craft, and examined. Their story helped popularize many of the classic abduction details still told today.

CASE 002

Travis Walton

1975 · Arizona

A logger in Arizona who said he was struck by a beam of light and taken for several days. His coworkers reported seeing the craft. The case later inspired the film Fire in the Sky.

CASE 003

Allagash Abductions

1976 · Maine

Four friends on a canoe trip in Maine claimed they were taken from their campsite. All four later described similar experiences under hypnosis.

04 / Perspective

Skepticism & alternative views

Many researchers point to sleep paralysis, false memories, cultural influence, and the power of suggestion—especially under hypnosis—as more likely explanations. No physical evidence accepted by the scientific community has ever confirmed that abductions by extraterrestrials have occurred.

Still, the stories continue to fascinate people and remain a major theme in books, documentaries, and popular culture.
